How the radar works

Nine simple steps from posts to a verdict.

  1. 1
    Listen

    Collect posts

    News articles and public social posts on each topic, refreshed every few minutes. The radar looks at the last 6 hours. Real account names are hidden.

  2. 2
    Read

    Understand meaning

    An AI model turns each post into its meaning, so the same message in other words still matches.

  3. 3
    Focus

    Stay on topic

    Posts about the topic count. Posts the search picked up by accident are ignored.

  4. 4
    Clean

    Remove the common part

    What everyone on the topic says anyway is taken out. Only what is unusual is compared.

  5. 5
    Compare

    Check every pair of accounts

    Did two accounts say the same thing within minutes? Only that counts.

  6. 6
    Threshold

    Stand out from normal

    A pair matters only if it is clearly above what is normal for that topic. Busy and quiet topics are judged fairly.

  7. 7
    Group

    Find the swarm

    Accounts linked this way form a group of 3 or more. Weak signals add up into a strong one.

  8. 8
    Decide

    Decide as posts arrive

    Each new post adds or removes evidence. The radar decides as soon as it is sure, with at most 2% false alarms.

  9. 9
    Double-check

    Rule out real news

    If people are just reacting to a news story or a meme that spreads everywhere, it is not flagged.

It flags accounts acting in sync, not opinions. The verdict comes from these steps, not from an AI chatbot.
